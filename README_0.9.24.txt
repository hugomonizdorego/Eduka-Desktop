Edukasaun Desktop Suite 0.9.24

All included components use the same version number:
- Eduka-Desktop 0.9.24
- Eduka-Panel 0.9.24
- Eduka-Menu 0.9.24
- Eduka-Settings 0.9.24

Changes in this patch:
- Eduka-Panel looks cleaner: the white box behind the tray icons is gone, the icons sit straight on the panel; only the clock keeps its frame, because it opens the Action Center.
- Panel styles: Long (the whole screen edge, like other desktops), Floating bar, Short (centered) and Dock (as long as its icons, task buttons as icons). Choose with right-click on Eduka-Panel → Panel Style (with small pictures) or in Eduka-Settings → Eduka-Panel → Panel style (with previews).
- Panel clock: seconds and a blinking colon can be switched on (right-click the clock, or Eduka-Settings → Eduka-Panel); only a new minute slides in.
- Network popup: Wired and Wireless switches, every Wi-Fi network with its signal in percent and a lock for secured networks. Click a network, type the password (with show/hide) and press Connect right in the popup — no extra window. Wrong passwords and errors are written in the popup; a connected network offers Disconnect.
- Sound popup: "Launch player" lists the installed music and video players (VLC, Celluloid, Rhythmbox, ...), then the volume with mute, the output device and Sound Settings.
- Battery popup: level and battery model, "Using battery power – 5 hours 33 minutes remaining" (or charging time), screen brightness, the power profile (Power Saver, Balanced, Performance with power-profiles-daemon) and Power Settings.
- Bluetooth popup: switch Bluetooth on or off, see paired and new devices, connect, disconnect or pair (pair, trust and connect) inside the popup, and search for devices. Right-click shows the usual Blueman commands (Bluetooth Manager, Send Files, Adapter Settings). Without a Bluetooth adapter the icon is hidden on Eduka-Panel and the tile in the Action Center.
- The Action Center keeps its own look; the new popups are only for the icons on Eduka-Panel.
- Recommends blueman and power-profiles-daemon.
- Translations for Portuguese, Indonesian and Tetun of the new texts.
- 0.9.25 will be the last 0.9 patch before 0.10.0.

The Tetun check against the Timor Aid dictionary follows when www.timoraid.org can be reached from the build environment.
Development continues toward 0.10.0 and the final version 1.1.12 (November 2026).
